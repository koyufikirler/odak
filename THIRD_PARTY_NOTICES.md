# Third-party notices

## EasyPrivacy

`rules/easyprivacy.json` is a declarativeNetRequest conversion of the
[EasyPrivacy](https://easylist.to/) filter list, maintained by the EasyList
authors.

EasyPrivacy is dual-licensed under:

- GNU General Public License v3.0 — <https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.html>
- Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported — <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/>

The list is bundled as data; it is not downloaded at runtime.

## Regional filter lists

The regional rulesets in `rules/reg_*.json` are declarativeNetRequest conversions
of the lists in [uBlock Origin's regions collection](https://github.com/gorhill/uBlock).
Each list remains the work of its upstream maintainers and keeps its original
license. Only rules that the browser's declarativeNetRequest engine can express
are converted; cosmetic and unsupported rule types are omitted.
